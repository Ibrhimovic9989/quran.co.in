# Third-party content

Apache-2.0 covers original project code, not third-party assets or services.

- Bundled KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs fonts retain the King Fahd Complex's terms.
  Do not assume they may be modified or sold under Apache-2.0.
- Quran text, translations, tajweed data, and streamed recitations retain
  resource-specific terms. Verify them before redistributing content.
- `apps/web/public/build-on-stack.mp4` includes third-party vocal audio.
  Redistribution permission has not been established in this repository.
  Obtain permission or replace the recording before redistributing the video.

This is a preliminary inventory, not a declaration that all assets are cleared.
Contributors must document sources and licenses for new assets.

# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and respectful community — one
worthy of a project centered on the Qurʾān — we as contributors and maintainers
pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex, level of experience,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or background.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps our community:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful to others.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them contribute.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the work.
- Showing adab — good character — in all interactions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Insults, demeaning comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Disrespecting the sanctity of the Qurʾān or using the project to demean any
  faith or people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove,
edit, or reject contributions, comments, and commits that violate them, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors they deem
inappropriate.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately — for example via a
GitHub private security advisory on this repository, or by direct message. All
reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion.
